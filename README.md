About

VUpload is a free file hosting service designed to make file sharing quick and effortless.

Upload your files anonymously, receive both a normal download link and a short link, generate a QR code instantly, and share your files from any device.

No registration required.

How It Works

1. Visit https://vupload.xo.je
2. Drag & drop your file or choose one manually.
3. Upload your file.

## FAQ

#### Is VUpload free?
Yes. VUpload is completely free to use.

#### Do I need an account?
No. Uploads are completely anonymous.

#### What is the maximum upload size?
100 MB per file.

#### How long are files stored?
Files are automatically deleted after 90 days.

#### What links do I receive?
- Normal download link
- Short link
- QR Code

#### Can I upload APK or EXE files?
Yes. VUpload supports APK, EXE, and many other common file formats.

#### Does VUpload work on mobile?
Yes. VUpload works on phones, tablets, and desktop browsers.
